# Cost risks and long-term trends affecting daily-menu restaurant profitability in Peru, 2019–2026 (outlook to 2030)

Research note, 15 Oct 2026. Scope: Peru only. Every figure has a source. Estimates and inferences are labelled.

Access limits: the BCRP statistics API (estadisticas.bcrp.gob.pe) and BCRP PDFs (Memorias, Reportes de Inflación, Notas de Estudios) were blocked from this environment (HTTP 403 / Incapsula). Most figures below therefore come from press reports of INEI/BCRP releases (Gestión, El Comercio, Forbes Perú, Finanzas Digital, Peru Retail, Infobae). Before calibrating the model, the report writer or analyst should pull the official BCRP series to fill the gaps: monthly IPC Lima "comidas fuera del hogar" and IPC by city/item.

## Q1. Annual inflation of food and "restaurantes / comidas fuera del hogar" (IPC), nationally and by city, 2019–2026

### Takeaway
Headline inflation peaked at 8.46% (Lima) / 8.56% (national) in 2022, with food at about 15%. "Restaurantes y hoteles" lagged: it rose less than food in 2022 (9.5–9.8%) but kept rising when food fell, at 6.46% in 2023, 2.88% in 2024 and 2.70% in 2025, while food was about 0–1.7%. 2026 brought a new supply/energy shock: national 12-month inflation was 4.17% in Sep-2026.

"Precio del menú en restaurantes" was the product that most influenced 2025 Lima inflation — [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/inflacion-en-el-2025-fue-de-151-los-rubros-que-variaron-de-precios-noticia/)

- **Estimate.** Approximate Lima headline cumulative 2019–2025, compounding 1.90 (2019), ~2.0 (2020, national 1.97), 6.43, 8.46, 3.24, 1.97 and 1.51: about +28%. This is a rough figure from mixed national/Lima annual rates.
- Southern cities (Cusco, Puno, Arequipa, Tacna) and Ica ran 2–3 pp above Lima at the 2023 peak. City-level spread is real and matters for a Puno/Juliaca model.

- City food-specific inflation (alimentos y bebidas by city) 2019–2025 was not found. Only headline city CPI for some years was found.
- Juliaca is not one of INEI's 26 IPC cities. Puno city is the closest proxy (not verified in an INEI list in this session).
- No full 2019 or 2020 restaurantes y hoteles figures were found.

## Q2. Price history and volatility of key ingredients (pollo, arroz, papa, aceite, cebolla, huevo, GLP) and shock episodes

### Takeaway
Ingredient prices are far more volatile than headline CPI. Single-item annual moves of ±15–60% are common, and moves above 100% occur (potato 2022). The worst cluster was 2021–2022: oil +63.5% in 2021, eggs +47% in 2022, potato +66 to +135% in 2022, GLP +51% in 2021. Most shocks partly reversed within 12–24 months. Restaurants' own menu prices did not reverse.

### Cited Findings
**2021 (national annual, INEI)** — [Forbes Perú](https://forbes.pe/economia-y-finanzas/2022-01-01/inflacion-en-2021-fue-la-mas-alta-de-los-ultimos-13-anos/)
- Aceite vegetal envasado +63.5%; pollo eviscerado +24.3%; cebolla china +42.6%; gas propano doméstico +51%; GLP vehicular +78%
- Chicken reached S/ 10/kg in Oct-2021 — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/america/peru/2021/10/04/precio-del-pollo-subio-a-10-soles-el-kilo-y-amas-de-casa-reclaman-por-incremento-que-afecta-su-economia-familiar/)

**2022 (national annual, INEI): global food/fertilizer shock** — [Finanzas Digital](https://finanzasdigital.com/inflacion-peru-cierre-ano-2022/)
- Papa huayro +135.29%, amarilla +130.39%, blanca +76.72%, color +66.67%; tubérculos group +33.0%
- Huevos de gallina +47.04%
- Aceites y grasas +14.3% (aceite vegetal envasado +11.2%)
- Cebolla de cabeza +16.7%; cebolla china −16.54%
- Pollo eviscerado −1.8% (after +24.3% in 2021)
- Gas doméstico envasado −9.20%; GLP vehicular −22.70%
- Potato +121% year on year in Oct-2022 (Midagri). Restaurateurs said most basic inputs were up more than 100% vs 2019 (lime S/ 1.80→6.50/kg) — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/tu-dinero/precios-de-menus-peruanos-en-restaurantes-suben-cuales-son-los-platos-que-mas-elevaron-su-valor-ceviche-pollo-a-la-brasa-lomo-saltado-arroz-chaufa-inflacion-rmmn-noticia/)

**2023 (Lima): blockades, El Niño costero, avian flu**
- Potato reversal: blanca −27.25%, color −20.88%, huayro −19.6%, amarilla −12.7%; chicken offal (menudencia) +12.1% — [Mercado Negro](https://www.mercadonegro.pe/actualidad/cuanto-aumentaron-los-precios-al-consumidor-durante-2023)
- Chicken peaked at up to S/ 13/kg in Lima markets in Jun-2023, from under S/ 10. The avicultores association blamed supply-demand imbalance, not a structural cause. Avian flu hit egg producers hard in 2021–22, leaving a shortage of laying hens — [Panamericana](https://panamericana.pe/nacionales/383991-precio-pollo-bajaria-siguientes-semanas-llegado-costar-s-13)
- El Niño / lime: lime then fell 49.6% in Nov-2023. Cebolla roja −21.6% and china −18.2%, huevos −4.2% that month — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2023/12/04/inflacion-en-peru-se-acerca-a-cerrar-el-2023-en-su-rango-meta-rubro-de-alimentos-registra-principal-reduccion/)
- In Jan-2023, during the blockades, 89% of agricultural products in Puno rose more than 10%, and Puno's monthly CPI was +3.0%. Pea prices exceeded S/ 10/kg in at least 10 regions, against S/ 6.8 at end-Dec — [El Comercio / IPE](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/inflacion-en-regiones-afectadas-por-protestas-tocaron-maximo-en-15-anos-noticia/)

**2024 (Lima annual): broad reversal** — [Peru Retail](https://www.peru-retail.com/?p=356845)
- Aceite vegetal envasado −14.4% (aceites y grasas −6.1%); huevos −17.7%; papa amarilla −30.4%; hortalizas y tubérculos −5.1%; condiments up (culantro +56.3%, ajo molido +44.5%)

**2025**
- Chicken and other meats rose; fish fell. No item percentages were given — [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/inflacion-en-el-2025-fue-de-151-los-rubros-que-variaron-de-precios-noticia/)

**2026: Camisea gas/energy shock + Middle East fuel shock + El Niño costero**
- In Mar-2026 the main Camisea pipeline ruptured in La Convención (Cusco). Restaurateurs reported beef up from S/ 30 to 34/kg, chicken up about S/ 2/kg and a higher balón price, and said they might raise the menú up to 15% — [Panamericana](https://panamericana.pe/24horas/locales/466364-restaurantes-descartan-subir-15-precio-menu-crisis-energetica)
- Pluspetrol halted GLP production. Bottlers were at minimum capacity, with stock covering about 30% of the lost supply. A balón increase of about 10% was expected, and Pisco loading times went from 14 h to 3–5 days — [Exitosa](https://www.exitosanoticias.pe/actualidad/crisis-camisea-precio-balon-gas-subiria-10-plantas-operan-minimo-n170401)
- The BCRP cited El Niño costero, higher fuel and a gas supply crisis tied to the Iran war as drivers — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/03/20/julio-velarde-del-bcrp-inflacion-podria-superar-a-la-de-eeuu-por-alza-en-precio-de-alimentos/)
- Sep-2026 (Lima, monthly): pollo eviscerado −4.6%; tomate +28.92%; vehicle fuels +5.2% — [Finanzas Digital](https://finanzasdigital.com/inflacion-peru-septiembre-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Estimate from the scattered annual points above.** Annual % changes found:
  - **Pollo:** +24.3 (2021), −1.8 (2022). The 2023–2025 annual figures were not found, but there was a Jun-2023 spike of about +30% intra-year (S/ <10 → 13). Rough SD of annual change: 15–20%.
  - **Huevo:** +47.0 (2022), −17.7 (2024). Rough SD: 20–30%.
  - **Papa:** +66 to +135 (2022), −13 to −27 (2023), −30 (2024 amarilla). Rough SD: 40–60%. This is the most volatile item.
  - **Aceite:** +63.5 (2021), +11.2 (2022), −14.4 (2024). Rough SD: 25–30%.
  - **Cebolla:** +16.7 / −16.5 (2022 by variety), −21.6 in a single month (Nov-2023). Rough SD: 20–30%.
  - **GLP doméstico:** +51 (2021), −9.2 (2022), about +10 expected (Mar-2026). Rough SD: 20–25%.
  - **Arroz:** no annual figure found. It is generally regarded as stable, but that is unverified.
- **Estimate.** Diversification matters. A menú ingredient basket blends 5–10 items, so basket volatility is much lower than single-item volatility. The "alimentos y bebidas" division itself ranged 0.06%–15.2% per year in 2019–2025, a SD of about 5 pp. A weighted ingredient-basket SD of about 6–9 pp/year is a reasonable model assumption (labelled estimate, not computed from microdata).
- Shocks typically mean-revert within 12–24 months: potato 2022→2023–24, oil 2021→2024, eggs 2022→2024. A model with mean-reverting (AR(1)) ingredient prices fits better than a random walk with drift.

### Gaps
- No annual figures were found for arroz or for pollo in 2023, 2024 and 2025. The MIDAGRI SISAP wholesale series and the BCRP item-level series were not reachable.
- No Puno/Juliaca-specific ingredient price series were found.
- No official GLP balón price series (Osinergmin Facilito) was retrieved.

## Q3. Remuneración Mínima Vital (RMV): history and expected path

### Takeaway
RMV went from S/ 930 to 1,025 (May 2022), to 1,130 (Jan 2025) and to 1,230 (Oct 2026), with S/ 1,300 agreed for the first half of 2027. That is about +40% from 930 to 1,300 over roughly 5 years. Increases are discrete, political and arrive every 2–3 years, recently in two-step tranches.

### Cited Findings
- RMV S/ 930 in 2020–2021 — [GeoVictoria](https://www.geovictoria.com/es-pe/blog/sueldo-minimo-en-peru). The S/ 930 level was set by an earlier decree (2018) — [LP Derecho](https://lpderecho.pe/incrementan-remuneracion-minima-vital-930-soles/). The exact date was not verified in this session.
- S/ 930 → 1,025 (+S/ 95, +10.2%) by D.S. 003-2022-TR, published 3 Apr 2022 and effective 1 May 2022 — [La Cámara](https://lacamara.pe/todo-lo-que-debe-saber-sobre-la-remuneracion-minima-vital/?print=pdf)
- S/ 1,025 → 1,130 (+10.2%) by D.S. 006-2024-TR, published 28 Dec 2024 and effective 1 Jan 2025 — [GeoVictoria](https://www.geovictoria.com/es-pe/blog/sueldo-minimo-en-peru); [LP Derecho](https://lpderecho.pe/aumentan-sueldo-minimo-1130-soles/)
- CNTPE extraordinary session No. 49 (3 Sep 2026): workers and employers agreed on S/ 1,300 — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=832039)
- First tranche S/ 1,130 → 1,230 (+8.8%) effective 1 Oct 2026 (D.S. 015-2026-TR). Second tranche +S/ 70 to 1,300 in 1H-2027 still needs a new decree — [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/peru/sueldo-minimo-en-peru-cuanto-sube-en-octubre-y-cuando-volvera-a-subir/)

### Inferences
- **Estimate.** RMV compound growth from 930 (2018) to 1,300 (2027) is about 3.8%/yr, above average inflation of about 3%/yr. Real labor cost rises.
- **Estimate.** Model RMV as a jump process: about a 10% step every 2–3 years, with probability about 0.35–0.45 per year. After 1,300 in 2027, the next step is plausibly 2029–2030, given the political cycle (new government 2026–2031). This is speculative.
- For a menú restaurant where labor is about 20–30% of cost (not verified here), a 10% RMV step raises total cost by about 2–3%. That shock is the same size as a full year of target inflation.

### Gaps
- No official MTPE table with exact dates before 2022 was retrieved, including the 850 → 930 step in 2018.
- No formal indexation rule exists. The CNTPE parties still disagree on the update method — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=832039)

## Q4. Pass-through: do menú prices follow cost inflation, and how fast?

### Takeaway
Menú prices adjust slowly and stickily upward. They lag food-cost shocks by about 6–18 months and do not come down when ingredient prices fall. In 2022 food rose 15% while restaurants rose about 9.5–9.8%. In 2023–2025 food rose 0–3.7% while restaurants rose 6.5%, 2.9% and 2.7%, catching up.

### Cited Findings
- 2022: food 15.22% vs restaurantes y hoteles 9.81% (national) — [Finanzas Digital](https://finanzasdigital.com/inflacion-peru-cierre-ano-2022/)
- Oct-2022: menú price +0.5% in the month while potato was +121% year on year. Central Lima menú went from S/ 8–10 (2019) to S/ 12 (2022), and menú ejecutivo to at least S/ 15 — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/tu-dinero/precios-de-menus-peruanos-en-restaurantes-suben-cuales-son-los-platos-que-mas-elevaron-su-valor-ceviche-pollo-a-la-brasa-lomo-saltado-arroz-chaufa-inflacion-rmmn-noticia/)
- 2023: food 3.74% vs restaurantes y hoteles 6.46% (Lima) — [Mercado Negro](https://www.mercadonegro.pe/actualidad/cuanto-aumentaron-los-precios-al-consumidor-durante-2023)
- 2024: food 0.06% vs restaurantes y hoteles 2.88% (Lima). Dec-2024 monthly: food −0.60% vs restaurants +0.19% — [Peru Retail](https://www.peru-retail.com/?p=356845)
- 2025: menú price was the top product driving Lima inflation, with restaurants +2.70% vs food +1.68% — [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/inflacion-en-el-2025-fue-de-151-los-rubros-que-variaron-de-precios-noticia/)
- Nov-2023: food −1.19% in the month while restaurantes y hoteles rose +0.26% — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2023/12/04/inflacion-en-peru-se-acerca-a-cerrar-el-2023-en-su-rango-meta-rubro-de-alimentos-registra-principal-reduccion/)
- Mar-2026: restaurateurs said they would raise the menú up to 15% only if the gas problem was not solved within days, which shows reluctance to reprice — [Panamericana](https://panamericana.pe/24horas/locales/466364-restaurantes-descartan-subir-15-precio-menu-crisis-energetica)

### Inferences
- **Estimate.** Restaurant-division pass-through in the shock year (2022) was about 0.6–0.65 (9.5/15.2). Cumulative pass-through over 2022–2025 is about 1.0 or slightly above (≈23% vs ≈22%). Repricing is asymmetric: it goes up with a lag and never comes back down.
- Profitability implication (inference): margins are squeezed in the first 6–12 months of a cost shock and then over-recover when ingredients revert while menú prices stay sticky. A Monte Carlo model should include (i) a pass-through lag, for example 50–65% in year t and the rest in t+1, and (ii) downward rigidity, meaning no menú price cuts.
- Menú prices move in discrete steps (S/ 1 jumps, e.g. S/ 10 → 12). In low-price markets like Juliaca, a S/ 1 step is about 8–12% at once. This is an inference; Juliaca menú prices were not found.

### Gaps
- No econometric study of Peruvian menú pass-through was found. A BCRP Nota/Revista Moneda item could not be read (blocked).
- No menú price series by city outside Lima.

## Q5. Macro and regional risks: social conflict, blockades, climate, and their impact on restaurant sales

### Takeaway
The south (Puno, Cusco) is Peru's highest-risk zone for conflict. The Dec-2022–Mar-2023 blockades cost Puno about S/ 46 M in tourism in two months, caused about 20,000 job losses and pushed city CPI above 11% year on year. No restaurant-specific sales loss figures were found. Climate (El Niño 2023, 2026) works mainly through ingredient prices.

### Cited Findings
- Defensoría Report No. 258 (Sep-2025): Puno had 19 social conflicts (16 active, 3 latent), second nationally after Loreto, with Cusco third — [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=400498)
- 197 conflicts registered nationally as of Apr-2025 — [Diario Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/politica/defensoria-del-pueblo-cuatro-nuevos-conflictos-sociales-en-abril-elevan-a-197-los-casos-reportados-gobierno-noticia/). Only the headline was seen; the article was not fetched.
- Jan-2023: MYPE sales losses of S/ 670 M in the interior. Puno tourism losses were S/ 46 M (Dec–Jan) with 20,000 jobs lost (Cámara Hotelera de Puno). Cusco lost S/ 3 M/day in tourism — [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/enero-registro-s-670-millones-de-perdidas-en-ventas-de-pymes-por-protestas-noticia/)
- Puno protests: losses of S/ 450 M per day (headline figure, likely the whole regional economy and probably overstated) — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-protestas-puno-generan-perdidas-economicas-s-450-millones-diarios-924200.aspx). The Cámara de Comercio de Puno estimated over S/ 166 M lost — [Exitosa](https://www.exitosanoticias.pe/exitosa-peru/camara-comercio-puno-advierte-region-pierde-mas-s166-millones-bloqueos-protestas-n92152). Only the headlines were seen; the articles were not fetched, and the dates and scope are unclear.
- Inflation in protest-affected southern regions averaged 10.7% vs 8.9% elsewhere (Jan-2023). Puno monthly CPI was +3.0% in Jan-2023 — [El Comercio / IPE](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/inflacion-en-regiones-afectadas-por-protestas-tocaron-maximo-en-15-anos-noticia/)
- Camisea pipeline rupture (Mar-2026) disrupted GNV/GLP supply and food transport — [Panamericana](https://panamericana.pe/24horas/locales/466364-restaurantes-descartan-subir-15-precio-menu-crisis-energetica)

### Inferences
- **Estimate.** For Puno/Juliaca, model a blockade event with annual probability of about 0.15–0.25 (major multi-week events in 2023, plus recurring shorter strikes), lasting 2–12 weeks. Effects: (i) a sales drop, magnitude unknown (no restaurant data; tourism-linked businesses were hit hardest); (ii) a local ingredient-cost spike of +10–30% on fresh produce (89% of agricultural products rose more than 10% in one month); (iii) GLP/fuel shortages. This is an assumption, not a measured parameter.
- Juliaca's daily-menu clientele is mostly local workers rather than tourists, so the sales impact may be smaller than Puno tourism losses. Blockades that cut supply (Juliaca is a transport hub) raise costs, though. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No documented restaurant-sector sales loss by region or event was found.
- Full Defensoría time series by region (2019–2026) was not retrieved.
- No quantified El Niño 2023 or 2026 impact on restaurant sales was found.

## Q6. BCRP and consensus inflation outlook 2026–2027 (and to 2030)

### Takeaway
After 1.3% in 2025, 2026 turned into an above-target year. The BCRP raised its 2026 forecast from 2.4% (Mar) to 3.8% (Jun-2026). It sees a return to about 2% in 2027. Surveyed agents expect 2.8–3.5% for 2026, about 2.25–2.6% for 2027 and 2.3–2.5% for 2028.

### Cited Findings
- BCRP Reporte de Inflación Mar-2026: 2.4% for 2026 and 2.0% for 2027 — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/03/20/julio-velarde-del-bcrp-inflacion-podria-superar-a-la-de-eeuu-por-alza-en-precio-de-alimentos/)

### Inferences
- Actual 12-month inflation (4.17% in Sep) is running above the June BCRP forecast (3.8%). The 2026 outcome is likely about 3.8–4.5%. This is an inference; the Sep-2026 Reporte de Inflación was not found.
- **Estimate.** For 2027–2030, use a baseline of 2.0–2.5%/yr (target center plus survey), with SD of about 1.5–2 pp. The 2019–2026 history ranges from 1.3% to 8.6% with 2 large shock years out of 8.

### Gaps
- The Sep-2026 BCRP Reporte de Inflación projections could not be retrieved.
- No official forecast exists for "comidas fuera del hogar" or food beyond 2027.
